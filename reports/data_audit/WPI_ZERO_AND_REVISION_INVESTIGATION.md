# WPI Zero and Revision Investigation

## Objective

Determine whether the six WPI commodity series with numeric zeros represent missing data, an official convention, or genuine zero-valued observations; and reconcile the April 2026 provisional-versus-later value discrepancy.

## Official sources reviewed

- OEA/WPI FAQ: <https://eaindustry.nic.in/uploaded_files/FAQs_on_WPI.pdf>
- OEA metadata: <https://eaindustry.nic.in/pdf_files/Metadata_WPI.pdf>
- April 2026 provisional release: <https://eaindustry.nic.in/archive_data/wpi_press_release_201112/summary_apr_2026.doc>
- Official 2011-12 workbook: <https://eaindustry.nic.in/indx_download_1112/monthly_index_202606.xls>
- OEA download page: <https://eaindustry.nic.in/download_data_1112.asp>

## Six zero-valued commodity series identified

The local workbook contains 505 zero values, all confined to the following six commodity rows:

- Peas (Green)
- Cauliflower
- Mango
- Orange
- Grapes
- Litchi

These rows are not random or spread across the sheet; they are a small subset of detailed commodity rows.

## Findings

- The local WPI workbook and the later official workbook match exactly.
- No official data dictionary reviewed in this audit defines zero as a missing value, not applicable, or unavailable observation.
- The value zero therefore remains a source value whose meaning cannot be established without a formal OEA clarification.
- Because an official zero convention was not found, zeros must be treated as numeric observations pending an authoritative statement.

## April 2026 discrepancy

The official April 2026 release states:

- All Commodities = 167.0 provisional
- The release is dated 14 May 2026
- Provisional figures are finalised after 10 weeks from the month of reference and frozen thereafter.

The later official OEA workbook contains:

- April 2026 All Commodities = 167.6
- This value matches the uploaded workbook exactly.

This is a documented publication-vintage difference: one is a provisional release value; the other is a later official workbook value. It is not a rounding issue. The evidence does not establish that 167.6 is formally final, only that it is the later official downloadable series and the uploaded workbook value.

## Conclusion

The six zero-valued rows are not automatically missing values. They remain numerical source values whose semantics are unresolved. The April 2026 difference is a documented provisional-versus-later vintage difference, not evidence of a local data error.
