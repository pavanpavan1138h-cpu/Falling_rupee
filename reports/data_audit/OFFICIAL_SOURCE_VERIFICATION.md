# Official Source Verification

## Scope and evidence standard

This review checks official source documentation against the eight files in `data/raw/`. Definitions below are verified from the cited official publications or series metadata; they do **not** authenticate every uploaded file. A matching series identifier, filename, sheet label, or coverage is not proof of download origin or historical vintage. Source pages were accessed on 2026-11-09. No raw file was modified.

## Source limitations that affect research claims

- The local USD/INR, Brent, repo, NEER/REER, and CPI files have no adequate source URL plus retrieval timestamp or vintage identifier. Official definitions do not fill those file-level provenance gaps.
- The repo-rate series requires a dated RBI/DBIE extract and its metadata before its month-end interpretation can be treated as a verified measurement convention.
- The NEER/REER methodology is verified, but a dated export is required to validate the uploaded values after the last period shown in the inspected RBI publication.
- CPI source publications verify the 2024-base series design and the stated general-index back-series scope, not the authenticity or vintage of the two local workbooks.
- WPI source and sheet-content agreement are verified. The current workbook contains revisions relative to the contemporaneous provisional release; the local vintage is not recorded. No source definition was found for the numeric zeros in six detailed series.
- No source material reviewed establishes sufficient historical release snapshots for strict real-time backtesting across all inputs.
